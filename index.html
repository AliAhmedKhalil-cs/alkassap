Executive Overview

A 34-Year Legacy of Excellence

Established formally in 2015, Al Kassap operates at the critical intersection of physical infrastructure, logistics, and digital advancement.

Under the leadership of Ahmed Khalil Gamil Kassap, the firm is built upon more than three decades of rigorous operational management. Beginning his career in elite hospitality, large-scale catering operations, and facility management across the UAE and Egypt, Mr. Kassap developed profound expertise in complex logistics, team leadership, and quality control. Today, this foundational discipline drives Al Kassap’s General Contracting, Life Support Services (الإعاشة), and Enterprise Digital Transformation divisions.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To execute physical infrastructure, general supplies, and technological architecture with uncompromising discipline, ensuring stability, scalability, and exact specification adherence for government and enterprise clients.

Vision: To serve as the definitive single-source executive partner in Egypt for institutions requiring structural development, operational catering/supplies, and enterprise-grade digital systems.
